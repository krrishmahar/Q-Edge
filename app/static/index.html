About Q-Edge

Q-Edge is a hackathon-scale prototype demonstrating tiled Quantum Hadamard Edge Detection (QHED).

QPIE — Quantum Probability Image Encoding

Each image tile is amplitude-encoded into a quantum state vector. Pixel intensities become the amplitudes of the superposition.

QHED — Quantum Hadamard Edge Detection

Hadamard gates applied to the encoded state produce forward-difference gradients Gx and Gy. The magnitude gives the edge response.

Tiling

Large images are split into T×T tiles with a 1 px halo. Each tile runs an independent circuit. Tiles are stitched back seam-free.

Honest note

At present this runs on a classical simulator. There is no quantum speedup over OpenCV. What is demonstrated is the correct quantum algorithm and its linear scaling with image size.
